# Mizan / Catering assets

All photographs are bundled progressive JPEGs, optimized locally (approximately 60–190 KB each). No image or font depends on a CDN. Estedad and Vazirmatn are the existing locally bundled Persian fonts.

## Provenance

- `hero.jpg`: new AI-generated illustrative banquet-table photograph for this redesign. Cream linen, navy napkins, canapés, clear water glasses; not a photograph of an actual Mizan event.
- `service-events.jpg`, `category-canapes.jpg`, `product-tarts.jpg`: optimized crops of that same generated photograph.
- `service-office.jpg`, `category-lunch.jpg`, `product-salad.jpg`: new AI-generated illustrative Iranian business-lunch photograph and crops.
- `service-meetings.jpg`, `category-break.jpg`, `product-fruit.jpg`: new AI-generated illustrative coffee-break photograph and crops.
- `story.jpg`: new AI-generated illustrative photograph of a caterer's hands arranging canapés in a kitchen. It is not a photograph of the sample business, its staff or a real event. The previous inherited hero is replaced; no undocumented stock photograph is added to this pack.

The photographs illustrate food categories and styling, not guaranteed quantities, packaging, staff or the precise composition of a purchasable product. Product descriptions state the sample portion/box contents. Owner verification is required for recipes, allergens and cross-contamination risk.

## Sample-data and workflow boundaries

Mizan is a fictional demo brand. Phone, address, recipes, prices and coordination hours are sample content and must be replaced before production. No client logos, fabricated review scores, event counts or guaranteed capacity are presented.

The browser-only catering planner prepares a plain-text brief. It makes no network request, saves no local/browser/server data, sends no message and creates no event, reservation, quote, contract or order. Copying is an explicit user action, with a selectable-text fallback when the clipboard API is unavailable. Capacity, delivery, equipment, staffing, pricing and event execution require separate human confirmation.

Online checkout is limited to individual pickup items. The pack creates zero dining tables and no instant-delivery zones. Advertised hours are **coordination hours**, not guaranteed event-service hours. A product's sample preparation time is not an event-booking lead time.
